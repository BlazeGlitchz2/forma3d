# Reference deconstruction — Forma / Material World

These are the **four actual attached screenshots**, in attachment order. The brief describes additional references, but their images were not supplied. Their written principles inform the direction; they are not claimed as visually inspected.

## 1. Pastel landscape / PeachWeb

1. **Focal point:** one suspended CGI subject, with an enormous landscape and reflection supporting it.
2. **Object/screen:** subject about 32% of width and 32% of height; the rendered environment occupies the whole frame.
3. **Type:** two large, light-weight lines at lower left, roughly 5% of screen width; small navigation at the top.
4. **Depth:** cropped glass sphere in foreground, subject in midground, rolling surfaces and a soft horizon behind; reflected geometry below.
5. **Colors:** lavender, blush, cool blue, warm white; dark purple utility text.
6. **Negative space:** exceptionally open upper half; the horizon gives the scene air.
7. **UI density:** low; controls live at edges and type sits directly on the world.
8. **Camera:** wide environmental shot, modestly low viewpoint, deliberate horizon.
9. **Motion:** likely gentle subject/camera displacement and slow reflected environmental movement. This is an inference from a still image.
10. **Forma principle:** render a ground, atmosphere and secondary physical forms; make an entire place rather than a product image.
11. **Do not copy:** fish, spheres as arranged here, logo, exact dunes, artwork, copy or navigation layout.

## 2. Red editorial / HAPE

1. **Focal point:** a close, dramatically cropped sculptural subject.
2. **Object/screen:** roughly half the width and two thirds of the height; foreground continues beyond the bottom edge.
3. **Type:** extremely heavy headline spans about 93% of width; small, bold utility text establishes scale.
4. **Depth:** large subject is in front of the typographic composition; red-lit space and light streaks behind it.
5. **Colors:** saturated red dominates; black headline; small off-white controls.
6. **Negative space:** space above and around the subject is intentionally occupied by type, with sparse open side regions.
7. **UI density:** small controls at the perimeter; no contained hero or commerce panels.
8. **Camera:** close portrait with a low, three-quarter perspective and confident cropping.
9. **Motion:** likely a weighted camera drift, restrained character movement, and environment lighting; no evidence of continuous viewer rotation.
10. **Forma principle:** the physical form becomes the layout. Huge type must pass behind real geometry; a scene has one dominant color.
11. **Do not copy:** ape, tattoos, red stage arrangement, brand, distinctive letterforms or exact layout.

## 3. Close sculptural environment / Altermind

1. **Focal point:** monumental rock-like geometry with a luminous interior aperture.
2. **Object/screen:** about 75% of the width and more than the full height; foreground occludes the environment.
3. **Type:** condensed white display type inside the aperture; an unusually small wordmark and technical instructions elsewhere.
4. **Depth:** cropped foreground mass, central opening, colored interior, cream environmental negative space.
5. **Colors:** warm paper, mineral grey, cyan/teal with small electric-blue and pink accents.
6. **Negative space:** quiet left-hand region balances dense geometry on the right.
7. **UI density:** extremely low; fine concentric annotations exist at much lower contrast than the subject.
8. **Camera:** very close to the geometry; monumental scale comes from cropping and perspective.
9. **Motion:** inferred orbital/parallax reveal through the opening; fine rings act as a stable reference.
10. **Forma principle:** move close enough to printed layers that they become an environment; avoid symmetrical product framing.
11. **Do not copy:** rocks, glowing aperture, concentric interface composition, brand or exact text treatment.

## 4. Grey virtual environment / Igloo

1. **Focal point:** a constructed dome with displaced blocks exposing light from inside.
2. **Object/screen:** structure about 50% of width and 48% of height; actual environment fills the frame.
3. **Type:** sparse, small monospace details in corners; the environment supplies identity.
4. **Depth:** detailed terrain in foreground, structure at mid-depth, several receding landscape planes with atmospheric haze.
5. **Colors:** almost monochrome slate and silver; white light establishes the focal point.
6. **Negative space:** sky and distant terrain keep the composition breathable.
7. **UI density:** very low; tiny annotations sit within the spatial scene.
8. **Camera:** environmental three-quarter perspective aimed at a physical construction.
9. **Motion:** inferred deliberate exploration and construction/deconstruction; annotations follow physical points.
10. **Forma principle:** build spatial continuity; object construction and manufacturing status can be states of one world.
11. **Do not copy:** dome, blocks, terrain, logo, numeric network or exact environment.

## Common decisions

- One renderer and one subject win over interface chrome.
- Scale contrast is more valuable than decorative components.
- Real geometry, print relief, shadows and lighting create depth.
- Main customer scenes have no product cards or boxed viewers.
- Native scrolling changes camera states; keyboard, touch and reduced motion remain usable.
